Boom Ragdoll: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Boom Ragdoll is a free browser game made by karbasha studio. This page explains what information this version of the game handles. It covers the version published through Microsoft (MSN Play and the Microsoft Store) and the game page at hamzamosleh.github.io/boom-ragdoll.

What the game collects

The game itself does not collect personal information. It does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or payment details, it has no chat, and it contains no analytics or tracking code of our own.

Saved progress

Your coins, upgrades and unlocks are saved in your browser's storage on your own device. They are not sent to us. You can remove them at any time by clearing the site data for the game's page in your browser.

Scores

When you play inside MSN Play, the game sends your best launch distance to Microsoft's leaderboard service through the MSN Play SDK. We do not receive your identity with it.

Advertising and the platform

When you play inside MSN Play, Microsoft shows the ads and operates the page around the game, including any sign-in. Microsoft may collect information for those purposes under its own terms. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement: https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement.

The game page on GitHub Pages is hosted by GitHub, which may log technical details such as IP addresses. See the GitHub Privacy Statement: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
